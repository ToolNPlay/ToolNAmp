Built for Pure Audio Performance

10-Band Graphic EQ & Preamp

Studio-grade biquad filtering spanning 31 Hz to 16 kHz with ±12 dB preamp gain and 18 one-click presets including Rock, EDM, Pop, Bass Boost, and Acoustic.

25 Festival & Retro Skins

Celebrate cultural festivals with Diwali, Holi, Dussehra, and Shivratri themes, alongside computing classics like Winamp 1998, The Matrix, Game Boy, and Amber CRT.

🌌

5 Real-Time 60 FPS Visualizers

Classic LED spectrum with dropping peak markers, glowing phosphor oscilloscope, 3D neon floor bars, 360° radial burst, and audio-reactive warp speed starfield.

📱

Screen-Locked Background Audio

Continuous background audio on Android and desktop. Keep jamming when your phone screen turns off, with native lock screen controls and headphone button support.

📁

Recursive Folder & Music Scanner

Scan your entire music directory in seconds. ToolNAmp parses ID3 metadata, embedded album art, bitrate, and duration into an instant search-as-you-type playlist.

🔒

100% Offline & Private

Zero telemetry, zero analytics, zero data uploads, and no mandatory login. Your audio files stay 100% on your device, private, and secure forever.
