Built Without Compromise

Every feature you loved from the golden era of desktop audio, modernized for speed and battery life.

🎛️
10-Band Graphic Equalizer
Audiophile-grade biquad filters spanning 32 Hz to 16 kHz. Includes 13 tuned presets (Rock, Pop, Bass Boost, Club, Jazz), live cubic spline frequency response curve, and master preamp control.
🌌
5 Real-Time 60 FPS Visualizers
Classic Winamp LED spectrum with dropping peak markers, glowing phosphor green oscilloscope, 3D neon floor bars, 360° radial burst, and audio-reactive warp speed starfield.
🎨
25 Curated Skins & Festival Themes
Celebrate festivals in style with Diwali, Holi, New Year, Christmas, Dussehra, and Shivratri themes, alongside computing classics like Winamp 1998, The Matrix, Game Boy, and Amber CRT.
📱
Screen-Locked Mobile Audio
Runs uninterrupted in the background when your phone screen is locked. Full lock-screen transport controls, headphone button integration, and MediaStore automatic music scanning.
🔍
1-Click Desktop Library Auto-Scan
Instantly indexes your local Music, Desktop, and Downloads folders recursively for all MP3, WAV, FLAC, M4A, and AAC files without freezing the player.
🪟
Window Shade & Always on Top
Press W to collapse the player into an ultra-compact 48px horizontal desk bar with live mini-visualizer, or press T to pin it above other windows while coding.
