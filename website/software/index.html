ToolNAmp — Retro Music Player

An ultra-lightweight, high-fidelity offline retro music player inspired by the golden era of Winamp. Re-engineered with modern Web Audio and native bindings, featuring 25 festival & retro skins, 10-band graphic equalizer with preamp, 5 real-time 60 FPS visualizers, and screen-locked background playback on mobile.
